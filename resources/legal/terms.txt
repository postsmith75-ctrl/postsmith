3. Description of Service
PostSmith is an artificial intelligence-powered content generation platform designed to assist users in transforming raw thoughts, ideas, and drafts into structured social media posts optimized for audience engagement. The Service includes, but is not limited to, the following features and functionalities:

Thought-to-Post Generation: AI-driven transformation of user-submitted raw thoughts into multiple formatted post variations across various social media platforms including, but not limited to, Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, Threads, and Reddit.
Draft Rewrite Engine: Analysis and structural improvement of existing user drafts with honest feedback and multiple rewritten versions.
Viral Lab: A community-driven analytics tool that analyzes high-performing social media posts to identify, catalog, and promote "engagement drivers" — psychological and structural patterns that correlate with high audience interaction.
Performance Tracker: A personal dashboard allowing users to save generated posts, log engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares), and receive data-driven insights about their highest-performing content formats.
Forge Library: A curated and community-discovered collection of engagement drivers used to guide the AI generation process.
Community Features: Leaderboards, contributor recognition, and shared discovery of new engagement patterns derived from anonymized and aggregated user submissions.
PostSmith does not guarantee any specific level of engagement, reach, virality, or commercial success from content generated through the Service. All generated content should be reviewed, fact-checked, and approved by you before publication.
